# Adjacent Product Tiers

Updated 2026-09-04.

Clarity Belongs participates in the shared adjacent-product expansion where the job is organization, knowledge, writing, planning, research, decision support, information cleanup, reference, or learning.

## Priority tiers for Clarity

1. Office add-ins
2. Local AI tools
3. Website widgets
4. Data tools
5. PWA and offline apps
6. Software templates and starter kits
7. Datasets and reference packs
8. Integrations and connectors
9. Reference and learning utilities
10. Scripts and automation where the outcome is information organization rather than system administration

## Shared-engine rule

Clarity may reuse factories from Software Belongs and AutoPilot IT, but product identity is determined by the user job. Clarity products should reduce cognitive load, organize information, improve understanding, or support deliberate decisions.

## First build wave

Start with the scripts-and-automation products above because they can share a simple local file-processing foundation and require little ongoing support.
