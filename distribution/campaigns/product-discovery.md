# Shared campaign: product discovery

## Use for
Clarity Belongs as one service, not 15 separate directory submissions.

## Mapped targets
- Product Hunt — https://www.producthunt.com/launch
  - Maker may submit their own product.
  - Requires a personal account; prepare product URL, tagline, media, description, first comment, and launch-day availability.
  - Do not ask people directly for upvotes.
- AlternativeTo — https://alternativeto.net/faq/
  - Use "Suggest new application"; verified email required; submission enters review backlog.
  - Position against website-change/uptime alternatives only where the comparison is truthful.
- SaaSHub — https://www.saashub.com/services/submit
  - Released products only; custom domain; English; approval required; listing competitors improves review.
- Uneed — https://www.uneed.best/
  - Re-verify current submission path immediately before execution.

## Readiness gate
Do not submit until production smoke, screenshots, canonical copy, privacy/contact, and attribution pass Priority 0.
