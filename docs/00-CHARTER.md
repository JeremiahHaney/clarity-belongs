# Clarity Belongs Charter

## Mission

Clarity belongs to everyone. Clarity Belongs helps people understand what changed, what matters, what needs attention, and what action to take next.

## Position in the ecosystem

Clarity Belongs is the **KNOW** identity in the three-site ecosystem:

- Software Belongs — DO
- Clarity Belongs — KNOW
- AutoPilot IT — HANDLE

Clarity is not the general utility catalog and is not a managed IT service. It is the awareness layer.

## What belongs here

Products that primarily:

- observe something over time
- detect changes
- preserve history
- compare before and after
- surface important differences
- notify the user when attention is warranted
- help the user understand recurring costs, availability, status, or risk

## What does not belong here

- one-time file transformation with no ongoing awareness value
- generic CRUD business apps
- high-touch managed services
- fear-based surveillance products
- products requiring heavy manual interpretation or customer support

## Product philosophy

The user should feel more informed and more in control after using Clarity, not more anxious.

Prefer calm summaries, evidence, history, and useful thresholds over constant notifications.

## Business model

- free where delivery cost is negligible
- paid where recurring checks, external providers, storage, notification volume, or other delivery costs are meaningful
- shared membership and account infrastructure with the wider ecosystem where practical
- no artificial feature withholding simply to manufacture upgrade pressure
