# Brand Identity

## Name

Clarity Belongs

## Core line

**Clarity belongs to everyone. We help you get there.**

## Role

Clarity Belongs is the ecosystem's KNOW identity.

Software Belongs helps people do things. Clarity Belongs helps people understand what is happening. AutoPilot IT handles technology for businesses that do not want to manage it themselves.

## Tone

Clarity should feel:

- calm
- useful
- plain-spoken
- empowering
- evidence-based
- non-adversarial

Avoid fear, alarmist security language, surveillance framing, and "fight/watchdog" branding.

## Primary identity

- Domain: `claritybelongs.com`
- Defensive domain: `claritybelongs.net`
- Social handle: `@claritybelongs`

## Messaging themes

- See what changed.
- Know what needs attention.
- Understand what you are paying.
- Track what matters.
- Compare before and after.
- Make better decisions.

## Navigation concept

The site should organize around outcomes and product families rather than exposing a giant monitor directory on the homepage.
